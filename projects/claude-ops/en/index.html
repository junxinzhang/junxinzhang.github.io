FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who operates Zaokit ClaudeOps?

Zaokit ClaudeOps is operated by an Australian registered company. Payments are processed securely by Stripe, and every payment comes with an official receipt and invoice.

What is the difference between 1x, 5x and 20x?

Each tier scales usage allowance, physical hardware tier, 1-on-1 concierge hours, and included perks:
• 1x Standard: Base usage allowance + Mac mini physical environment + 7×8h 1-on-1 dedicated support;
• 5x Pro: 5× usage allowance + high-spec Mac mini physical environment + 7×16h priority 1-on-1 support;
• 20x Ultimate: 20× usage allowance + high-spec Mac Studio / Mac mini physical environment + top-tier 7×24h round-the-clock private concierge channel + complimentary Google Drive 6 TB cloud storage.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose 1x for everyday chat and writing, 5x if you code with Claude Code every day, and 20x for multiple agents or heavy round-the-clock use. Not sure? Start lower and upgrade any time.

How do I actually use it? Remote Mac or local?

Both work. Use whichever suits you:
1. Remote desktop (VNC): connect to your dedicated high-spec Mac Studio or high-spec Mac mini with macOS Screen Sharing, RealVNC, TigerVNC or similar. You get the full macOS desktop and terminal, ready to go, and long tasks keep running.
2. Seamless local workflow integration: collaborate with the remote development environment directly from your local workflow and dev tools, with no local environment set-up or maintenance overhead. The remote Mac becomes your dedicated compute back end.

How are my code and data kept secure and private?

Every customer gets a dedicated physical high-spec Mac Studio or high-spec Mac mini on a single-tenant model, with no shared containers. Your session history, source code and environment variables stay on your own instance. You set your own access passwords and encryption, and our team only handles connectivity and billing.

Where are the machines and networks located?

Our physical high-spec Mac Studio and Mac mini machines are located in Australia, the United States, Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, Cambodia, New Zealand and Canada, connected via local native ISP broadband networks (not shared public cloud data centers) for maximum reliability. Pick your preferred region at checkout, and we assign you a fixed machine for the long term.

How does Google Drive & iCloud+ cloud storage work?

We offer 2 TB ($8.99/mo), 6 TB ($28.99/mo), 12 TB ($58.99/mo) and 30 TB ($148.99/mo) plans. At Stripe checkout, choose Apple iCloud+ or Google Drive (Google One) and note your account email; our 7×16h 1-on-1 concierge team handles activation and ongoing renewals.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Yes. Changes apply from your next billing cycle. If you need an upgrade straight away, contact us and we will charge the difference.

What if something goes wrong?

Contact us straight away. We prioritise troubleshooting and get you back up and running as fast as possible.

Which payment methods do you accept? Can I get an invoice?

We accept online subscriptions via Stripe, including Visa, Mastercard, Amex, UnionPay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link, billed in AUD, USD, EUR, NZD, CAD or SGD. Stripe issues an official receipt and invoice automatically. For multi-account business plans, email us.
